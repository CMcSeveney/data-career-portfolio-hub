## Project Talking Points

### SaaS Support Intelligence Dashboard

- Business problem: support teams need visibility into ticket volumes, SLA breaches, escalations and customer impact.
- Tools: SQL Server, SSMS, Power Query, Power BI and GitHub.
- Talking points: KPI choices, SLA validation, customer impact view, data-quality issues and how Support/Product/Engineering could use the output.

### Python Ticket Analytics Engine

- Business problem: manual ticket reporting can be slow, inconsistent and error-prone.
- Tools: Python, pandas, logging, pytest and GitHub.
- Talking points: reusable script design, validation checks, logging, scheduling and safe sample data.

### API Integration Troubleshooter

- Business problem: SaaS support teams need to diagnose whether API failures are caused by authentication, payloads, missing resources, rate limits or server issues.
- Tools: Postman, Bruno, Python requests, JSON and REST APIs.
- Talking points: 401 vs 403, safe API key handling, successful vs failed request comparison and developer-ready escalation evidence.

### Modern ETL Warehouse

- Business problem: raw operational data needs to be transformed into reliable reporting-ready warehouse tables.
- Tools: SQL Server, T-SQL, Python, SSMS, Power BI and Airflow/dbt awareness.
- Talking points: staging layer, fact table grain, dimensions, validation, reconciliation and production monitoring.

### Customer Churn Prediction

- Business problem: a business wants to identify customers at risk of leaving and understand churn drivers.
- Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib and Jupyter.
- Talking points: baseline model, confusion matrix, precision/recall/F1, threshold tradeoffs, leakage prevention and business recommendations.

### PySpark Retail Analytics

- Business problem: retail transaction data may become too large for pandas-only workflows.
- Tools: PySpark, Databricks, Python and Spark DataFrames.
- Talking points: Spark vs pandas, lazy evaluation, invalid transaction handling, aggregation and scaling considerations.

### AI Ticket Classification Assistant

- Business problem: support teams need help categorising tickets, summarising issues and routing work efficiently.
- Tools: Python, NLP preprocessing, scikit-learn or Hugging Face, FastAPI, Postman and GitHub.
- Talking points: label choices, model errors, misclassification review, privacy, hallucination risk, monitoring and human review.
